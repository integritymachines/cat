# Measuring Consciousness: Scaffold

Version 0.2, 12 November 2026. Baseline: the research note for Paper 4 and the summary report on the lower bound. Kernel: `0_LSMN_kernel.md` §4. Tags as in the kernel. Numbers from the note are unchecked. The fact check happens outside this series of drafts.

Headline. Observation from without cannot reproduce experience within. Control at a critical limit nevertheless consumes metabolic resources and produces control work, and both leave traces that can be recorded. The paper states which traces the operators of Paper 3 predict, where consciousness is present, how its degree varies, and what would refute the series.

Status of the paper. Scientific. It states tests with a refutation condition.

## 1. Thesis

- Claim: scientific observation is a second lossy translation. Proposition 3.3 of Paper 3.
- Claim: by Proposition 3.2, an agent that meets a collapsed gap or an unliftable route has to spend resources on control.
- Claim: either side of the conversion, resources consumed or work produced, leaves a trace.
- Dependency: the position on the hard problem in Paper 1.

## 2. Traces

- Metabolic trace: estimate of allocation. Candidates (note): regional hemodynamics, pupil diameter, autonomic balance.
- Control-work trace: spectral and kinematic. Candidates (note): graph reconstructions from multi-channel recordings, midfrontal theta, decision boundary, response-time dynamics.
- Efficiency: work per allocation. It separates states in which the two decouple (flow against overload).
- Assumptions 4.1 and 4.2 of the kernel. The inequality between metabolic input and torsion is taken from the note.

## 3. Dictionary

- Table: each operator of Paper 3 and its candidate estimator.
- Supports named in the note, to be fact-checked: separation of territory and map as orthogonal subspaces in hippocampal recordings, connectivity dimensionality and participation ratio, contraction against aliasing at the boundary of ignorance, multiplexing of dominant and suppressed percepts.
- Claim: the dictionary is the weakest link. Every test below is conditional on valid estimators.

## 4. Presence

- Lower bound: the engine pays only when the viability condition of Paper 3 holds.
- Motility and sessility: the first dividing line. A changing environment met in motion explodes combinatorially. A sessile agent can produce physical conjectures, as in literal branch and bound. A motile agent has to produce virtual ones [ruled].
- Boundary test, three points [ruled]: compression from territory to map, an outwardly measurable discontinuity at a critical limit, offline conjecturing in high-intensity REM phases.
- Claim [ruled]: only organisms that dream can be conscious. A conscious organism has to recuperate virtually as well as physically.
- Comparative table (summary report, unchecked): plants and microbes, insects, reptiles, cephalopods, mammals and birds.
- Machines [ruled]: current LLMs fail by lack of recalibration in runtime and lack of parochialism.
- Test 4.3: anesthesia and dreamless sleep.
- Dependency: a satellite note carries the comparative work.

## 5. Degree

- Test 4.4, spectral: step drop of the gap at a critical ambiguity, then nonlinear rise of control markers.
- Test 4.5, kinematic: response time diverges as the gap approaches its critical level.
- Test 4.6, braking: elevation of the decision boundary in proportion to torsion once the gap has collapsed. Vicarious trial and error at forks.
- Test 4.7, metabolic: allocation linear in the torsion shift, with slope inverse to efficiency.
- Confidence margin and individual differences (note): baseline critical level and efficiency differ between individuals.
- Change from the note [ruled]: nine hypotheses become presence and degree tests. Their content is kept.

## 6. States

- Table of the kernel: drift, overload, flow, meditation, psychedelics, propofol, ketamine and REM.
- Claim: the quantitative form of the altered-states section of Paper 1.
- Dreaming: actuators gated, participation ratio above wake, bounded deformation between wake and sleep, gap restored after sleep (note).

## 7. Agents

- Claim: two agents hold maps from private territories. Coordination proceeds under drift while the distance between their maps stays below a critical level.
- Claim: above it, coordination requires explicit translation at the boundary.
- Support (note, unchecked): compatibility is predicted by relational geometry and not by similarity of attributes.
